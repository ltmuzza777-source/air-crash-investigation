Status: Working draft; technical and timeline review pending.
Availability: All excerpts are supplied in the initial evidence package. None are requestable.
Gaps represent omitted uneventful recording, not missing or unavailable evidence.

Date: To be assigned, 2012
Time standard: Local time
Departure and intended destination: Hobart–Melbourne

### Speaker key
- CAPT — Captain, pilot flying.
- FO — First officer, pilot monitoring.
- GND — Hobart Ground.
- TWR — Hobart Tower.
- APP — Approach controller.
- CABIN — Lead cabin crew member, via interphone.
- SOUND — Recorded non-speech sound.
- AUTO — Automated aircraft announcement.

Only intelligible speech is transcribed. Radio transmissions received in the cockpit are included.

## Author-only notes — exclude from player evidence

Dialogue, timings, weather, and clearances are fictional. The hydraulic escalation and delayed warning presentation draw on the reference incident; exact checklist wording and landing configuration remain subject to verification. This is game evidence, not an operational checklist.

Technical reference: https://aerossurance.com/safety-management/a320-hydraulic-incident/

- No dialogue identifies the ruptured hose: the player must establish that through other evidence.
- The crew’s go-around concern is expressed as a belief, not a proven aircraft limitation.
- Exact warning labels, configuration, radio phraseology, altitude callouts, and brake/reverse behaviour still need checking.
- Final timestamps must agree with the flight-data reconstruction, particularly the unusually long float and landing roll.
- Passenger count and fuel response are omitted pending those decisions; they should be added later.
- Everyone’s survival will be confirmed in the incident summary, not inferred solely from the cabin sweep.
